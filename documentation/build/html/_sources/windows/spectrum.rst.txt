Spectrum
=============

In this section you can find how to use the spectra chart visualization window. 

The spectrum menu allows you to view the spectrum of the selected data point or region. 
It shows how much each element reacts to X-ray beam. 
This allows you to identify and analyze the elemental composition of the pigments used in the painting in detail. 

This menu has a few options which are discussed in detail below.

Global Average Spectrum
------------------------
The global average spectrum is the spectrum of the average intensity per energy bin accross the entire painting. 
The energy bins are computed between the low and high boundaries and with the bin size specified upon loading the raw file.

The spectrum can be made visible on the chart by ticking the **Global average checkbox**. 
Note that the global average spectrum shows up as a blue line on the generated spectra chart.


Selection Average Spectrum
--------------------------
When making a selection on the painting with the area selection tool, the average spectrum of the selection will be computed.
This is the spectrum of the average intensity per energy bin accros the selected part of the painting.
The energy bins are computed between the low and high boundaries and with the bin size specified upon loading the raw file.

The spectrum can be made visible on the chart by ticking the **Selection average checkbox**.
Note that the selection average spectrum shows up as a green line on the generated spectra chart.


Element theoretical graph and peaks
------------------------------------
The spectra chart also allows the visualization of the theoretical spectrum and intensity peaks of an element.
The theoretical intensity peaks are represented as ines at their corresponding energy values. 
The spectrum is the theoretical intensity of the element and excitation energy per energy bin, retrieived from the xrf4u and xraydb libraries.
The energy bins are computed between the low and high boundaries and with the bin size specified upon loading the raw file.

The theoretical graph can be made visible on the chart by ticking the **Element theoretical graph checkbox**.
Note that the element theoretical graph shows up as an orange line on the generated spectra chart.

The theoretical peaks can be made visible on the chart by ticking the **Element theoretical peaks checkbox**.
Note that the element theoretical peaks shows up as gray vertical lines on the generated spectra chart.

Theoretical spectrum properties
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
To make a theoretical graph or peak appear, the theoretical spectrum properties need to be entered.
An element can be selected in the **element menu**. 
When clicked, the menu opens a periodic table. 
In this table elements which are present in the project's elemental data are highlighted with a yellow border.
An excitation energy can be set in the **excitation input field**.


Spectra chart options
----------------------
When the **Open Popup** button, depicted with the expand icon, is clicked, the spectra chart is expanded so data can be explored more easily.
You can als zoom in on the spectra chart and move the chart along the x and y-axis to more clearly look at certain parts of the chart. 
It is possible to reset this to return to the chart as it was first generated.
